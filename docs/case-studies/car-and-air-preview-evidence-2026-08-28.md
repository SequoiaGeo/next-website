## Measurement boundaries

- The July 13 through July 31 figures and the July 13 through August 11 figures overlap. They must not be added together.
- The four phone leads in the July report use the report's platform label. They are not presented as four independently verified customers.
- The one paid job was separately reported by the client. No customer, vehicle, service, job value, or client revenue is disclosed.
- The client attributed the paid job to advertising, but the ad, call, and job records have not been independently joined into one trace.
- The call action was changed prospectively. The new Count One and 60-second rules do not rewrite historical events.
- A direct form test proves event delivery. It is not an ad-attributed form lead.
- The account was off before relaunch. No percentage increase, multiple, or inflated before-and-after claim is valid.
- The engagement is active. One paid job is an early result, not a repeatable trend or causal proof.
