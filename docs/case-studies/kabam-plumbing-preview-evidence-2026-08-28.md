## Excluded claims

- No current lead growth, confirmed-inquiry growth, booked-job growth, revenue, return, or AI-search gain is claimed.
- The page does not say 116 completed calls. It defines the metric as profile call-button clicks.
- The May and June Search Console snapshots are not charted as a trend and are not placed after the August release as though they resulted from it.
- The page does not say rankings were retained, the site indexed cleanly, all crawler behavior was verified, or every technical issue is fixed.
- Testimonial financial figures, call-volume figures, hiring claims, and a private referral are not repeated as Sequoia results.
- No claim says Next.js is a poor platform. The case-study conclusion is limited to the evidence threshold for this site at that time.

## Interpretation controls

- The hero proof strip contains attributable implementation evidence, not the client-earned review series or historical contact metric.
- The 5.0 rating and review count appear in a section labeled client context, with an explicit statement that KABAM earned the reviews through its customer work.
- The trust section says what the client described in the approved testimonial. It does not claim a measured trust score or identify the prior agency.
- The URL Inspection language remains limited to one tested page.
- The technical decision is phrased as a repair-first choice supported by the evidence available. Replatforming remains a valid option for a different site or later evidence.
- The open-items paragraph says known open issues include, rather than implying a complete defect inventory.
