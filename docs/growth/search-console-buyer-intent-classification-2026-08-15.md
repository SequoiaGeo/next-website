# Sequoia GEO Search Console Buyer-Intent Classification

Prepared: 2026-08-15

Source: Sequoia GEO Search Console property, Search results, July 17 through August 13, 2026. Read directly in-account on August 15, 2026.

## Purpose

Test whether Sequoia GEO's organic visibility is reaching plausible business buyers before treating impressions as an addressable qualified-traffic pool or changing industries.

## Method

The visible top 50 query rows were classified manually from the query wording:

- `In-ICP commercial`: the query explicitly seeks an SEO, marketing, GEO, or agency service relevant to Sequoia GEO.
- `Branded`: the query names Sequoia GEO or Aaron Husak.
- `Informational`: the query asks for information without a clear agency-selection signal.
- `Ambiguous`: the query does not reveal enough intent to classify reliably.

Industry clusters were assigned by the named vertical. General California and Fresno agency searches were placed in `General/local`. Queries containing `geo` were placed in `GEO/AI`. Weighted average position is impression-weighted within each cluster.

This classification covers the top 50 rows only. It represents 49,023 of 130,764 total impressions, or 37.5%, and 44 of 120 clicks, or 36.7%. It must not be presented as the intent mix of every query in the property.

## Decision

Do not pivot Sequoia GEO away from home services during the initial 30-day test. The visible query mix is overwhelmingly aligned with commercial agency demand. The immediate constraint is capture: rankings, title and snippet relevance, and the path from a qualified landing visit to a measured lead.

Run the roofing and plumbing service-page refreshes as staggered, pre-registered treatments after the seven-day measurement baseline. Publish plumbing first because it already demonstrates stronger click capture, then publish roofing one week later after its title-link, snippet, device, and SERP fit have been reviewed. The page mapping in `docs/growth/search-console-page-query-map-2026-08-15.md` confirms that `/roofing-seo` owns the core roofing service queries, while the plumbing service and comparison pages overlap on several service terms.

Preserve the plumbing comparison page during the first treatment and measure the plumbing query cluster across both URLs. Bundle internal links with the corresponding page treatment. Keep HVAC content and inbound links unchanged as the comparator. Do not make HVAC the first SEO wedge unless fresh Keyword Planner or query-level page evidence overturns the current ranking disadvantage.

## Limits

- Search Console impressions are not qualified visits, leads, or customers.
- Query wording identifies likely intent, not the identity or quality of the searcher.
- Average position is an aggregate across devices, locations, and search appearances.
- The top 50 rows do not represent the remaining 62.5% of impressions.
- Keyword Planner volume and bid evidence remain pending because Google Ads is blocked by the current ad-blocker setting.
