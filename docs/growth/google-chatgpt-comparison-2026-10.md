Aaron approved USD 500 media for Google Ads and USD 500 media for ChatGPT Ads, USD 1,000 combined. This is a channel comparison, not a randomized experiment. Different auctions, placements, queries, audiences and attribution windows prevent a causal claim about which platform is universally better.

## Capture and measurement

The form reuses /api/contact, its honeypot and validation, and its durable email/CRM acceptance contract. paid_snapshot_google and paid_snapshot_chatgpt are recognized by the API and CRM evidence formatter. Business website and optional email consent retain the existing assessment behavior. Invalid, failed, honeypot, synthetic or malformed acceptance responses must not emit a lead conversion. A repeated lead ID in one browser session must not emit twice.

Primary operational result: a unique accepted Snapshot request, followed by manual qualification and outcome reconciliation. generate_lead and OpenAI lead_created are the accepted capture events. They are not qualified prospects or signed clients. Source path and UTMs are evidence, not proof of person identity. Keep tests, duplicates, existing-client requests and outreach-assisted traffic separate. Cross-session duplicate submissions and company duplication require ledger reconciliation.

Google Calendar iframe views and focus/fallback clicks are diagnostic interaction events only. No browser event from this embed proves a completed booking. A completed booking requires a calendar record and identity/source reconciliation. Report accepted requests, qualified inquiries, booked meetings, held meetings and signed clients separately. Do not use overall GA4 keyEvents as the lead total because other account events include clicks and downloads.
