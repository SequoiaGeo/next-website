## Verified home-services evidence

Source: Sequoia GEO Search Console, July 17 through August 13, 2026, read August 15.

- Roofing SEO: 33,265 impressions, 5 clicks, average position 13.7.
- Plumbing SEO: 20,477 impressions, 5 clicks, average position 16.4.
- Both pages appear for direct commercial variants such as `roofing seo company`, `roofing seo agency`, `plumber seo services`, and `plumbing seo company`.
- The two pages have existing ranking history and can be improved without building a market from zero.

The current weakness is not market relevance. It is that most impressions occur below the high-click positions, and the existing pages have mixed offer language and limited click-through.

## Verified AI-search evidence

Source: `Hicks-Paving-AI-Search-Opportunity-Brief-2026-08-13.md`, based on the August 13 call transcript and client record.

- A paving contractor reported finding Sequoia GEO by asking an AI product whom to use for AI search.
- The inquiry progressed to accepted project work.
- The actual deliverable was not generic content production. It was entity clarification, owned-asset correction, public-source reconciliation, and a fixed before-and-after prompt panel.
- The event validates a discovery path and offer, not a repeatable conversion rate.

The strongest product language is therefore:

> Find out whether search engines and AI systems can identify, trust, and recommend the correct version of your business, then correct the evidence they rely on.

Do not promise an AI recommendation. Promise a sourced diagnostic, corrections to controlled assets, documented third-party submissions, and a measurable follow-up.

## Verified multi-location healthcare evidence

Sources:

- `UHC-WIC-Proposal-Scope-Assumptions-2026-08-14.md`
- `C:\Users\Aaron\Documents\SequoiaGeo\UHC-WIC-Thursday-Prep-2026-08-13.md`

The opportunity contains a real and repeatable operational problem:

- The organization does not know who controls multiple public location entities.
- Published location data, legacy profiles, and organization ownership can conflict.
- Some departments may qualify for separate profiles while others must be represented under a parent location.
- Location data needs an authoritative record, an access register, approval ownership, and a maintenance process.

This is a valid offer category. It is not yet evidence that healthcare organizations search frequently for an agency using the terms Sequoia currently targets. Procurement dependencies also make it less likely to produce multiple new customers inside 30 days.

Recommended secondary offer name:

`Multi-Location Listing Ownership and Accuracy Project`

Recommended buyer-led framing:

> We identify every public location entity, establish who should own it, correct approved information, document blocked or duplicate profiles, and leave your organization with a governance record it controls.

## 30-day targeting plan

### Primary audience

Established plumbing, roofing, HVAC, paving, restoration, and other home-service companies that:

- Already spend on marketing
- Cannot connect leads to jobs booked
- Have conflicting listings, tracking, agency reports, or public business identities
- Want direct access to an experienced operator

### Primary entry offers

1. Marketing Baseline Review for companies uncertain what their current marketing produces.
2. AI Recommendation and Entity Diagnostic for companies with conflicting or incomplete public evidence.
3. Focused verification engagement for a company that wants one suspected marketing problem proved and corrected.

### Secondary account list

Build a small account-based list of multi-location healthcare, franchise, and regional service organizations exhibiting public ownership or location-data contradictions. Send each prospect one verified issue and offer the bounded ownership-and-accuracy diagnostic. Do not mass-produce a healthcare campaign until the demand gate passes.

## Industry expansion gate

Create a dedicated second-industry landing page only when all five conditions are satisfied:

1. Fresh national Keyword Planner data shows meaningful commercial search demand.
2. Search-result sampling identifies a page type Sequoia can credibly outperform.
3. Sequoia has one relevant proof asset or a clearly bounded diagnostic deliverable.
4. The expected sales cycle can contribute inside the current cash horizon.
5. Building the page will not displace higher-confidence work on existing page-two home-service pages.
