## Hypothesis

A relevant, independently controlled podcast page that accurately identifies Aaron Husak as the founder of Sequoia GEO and describes his work in AI-search visibility for home-service businesses may make that positioning easier to verify than another self-published Sequoia page.

This is an authority experiment, not a backlink purchase and not a guarantee of retrieval or recommendation.

The episode is relevant because it already covers Aaron's home-services operating background and move into AI search. ## One requested change

Do not request keyword stuffing, an invented endorsement, a paid link, or language claiming that the podcast recommends Sequoia.
