# Sequoia GEO authority distribution system

Status: working plan

Prepared: 2026-08-20

## Authority principle

Sequoia should publish fewer claims about expertise and more artifacts that another person can inspect, reproduce, cite, or challenge.

## Publication order

1. Correct contradictory public claims.
2. Publish the AI search measurement methodology.
3. Apply the method to one bounded home-services pilot.
4. Publish the pilot results, including misses and variance.
5. Pitch the original data to independent sources.
6. Release the observation schema and codebook on GitHub.
7. Turn new account observations into short field notes only when the evidence is complete.

## Case-study gate

The current AI-search readiness field note remains anonymized.

Convert it into a named case study only after all of these are true:

- Written permission names the business and approved facts.
- The initial public contradictions are source documented.
- Completed corrections are separated from pending or rejected corrections.
- Post-change observations use the public measurement method.
- The page does not imply the corrections caused a recommendation without sufficient evidence.
- Customer identity, licensing, and legal language receive a final accuracy review.

## Independent corroboration targets

Prioritize sources capable of citing original research:

1. Home-service trade publications and newsletters.
2. Contractor associations and regional trade groups.
3. Field-service software and marketing technology partners.
4. Home-service business podcasts.
5. Local-business and search-industry publications.
6. Conference and webinar programmers.

Do not pitch a generic interview about GEO. Pitch a finding, the method, why it matters to operators, and the downloadable evidence.

## Research pitch structure

- Subject: one specific finding, not a service offer.
- Opening: the counterintuitive result in one sentence.
- Evidence: sample size, platforms, market, date, and limitation.
- Relevance: what an operator should verify or stop assuming.
- Offer: raw methodology, chart, or interview with Aaron.
- Disclosure: Sequoia is an AI SEO agency and produced the research.

## Field-note queue

### Ready after account verification

1. What Bing's AI citation report showed Sequoia this month.
2. Why an AI citation is not a recommendation.
3. Where comparison and statistics pages appeared in Sequoia's visibility reports.

### Ready after a client change log exists

4. What changed after correcting a conflicted business entity.
5. What remained unresolved after the public record was corrected.

### Ready after controlled platform observations

6. Where ChatGPT and Google disagreed about the same contractor.
7. How often a primary local-business recommendation repeated across five runs.

## Field-note evidence template

Every field note must state:

- The observed question.
- The property, business, or sample.
- The source system.
- The date range and read date.
- The exact metric definition.
- What was changed, if anything.
- What happened afterward.
- What cannot be concluded.
- A link to the public methodology.

## Outreach release gate

Do not begin a media pitch until the public report, methodology, corrections policy, and downloadable evidence all resolve without authentication and render correctly on mobile.
